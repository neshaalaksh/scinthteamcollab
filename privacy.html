Privacy Policy

Teamspace (https://workspace.scinth.co), operated by Scinth. Last updated: 7 October 2026.

What Teamspace is

Teamspace is a private workspace for the Scinth team and its clients. It provides tasks, a calendar, documents, spreadsheets, call requests and a shared file area (the Drive tab). Access is by invitation only: a person can sign in only if the workspace owner has added their email address.

Information we collect

  • Google account details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use these to identify you, check that you were invited, and show your name to teammates.
  • Content you create. Tasks, calendar events, documents, sheets, notes, call requests and similar items you add to the workspace.
  • Files you upload. Files you upload in the Drive tab, along with their name, type, size and the space they belong to.
  • Technical data. Basic logs kept by our hosting and database providers, such as timestamps and IP addresses, used for security and reliability.

How we use Google user data

Teamspace uses Google sign-in to confirm who you are. Teamspace also uses the Google Drive API so that files you upload in the Drive tab are stored in a Google Drive folder owned by the workspace owner. Specifically:

  • We create, name and delete files in that folder only when you upload or delete a file in Teamspace.
  • We store a reference to each file (its Drive file ID, link, name, type and size) so it can be listed in the workspace.
  • We do not read, scan or analyse the contents of your files, and we do not access Drive files that were not uploaded through Teamspace.
  • The Drive connection is a single storage account used by the workspace server. Other users do not grant Teamspace access to their own Google Drive.

Teamspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Limited Use

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, except as needed to provide the service (our hosting and database providers), to comply with law, or as part of a business transfer with notice to users.
  • We do not use Google user data to train machine learning or AI models.
  • Humans do not read Google user data unless you give permission for specific items, it is needed for security or to comply with law, or it is aggregated and anonymous for internal operations.

Who we share data with

Within the workspace, other members see your name and the content you add to spaces they can access. Clients (guests) see only the spaces they are invited to. We rely on these service providers to run Teamspace: Supabase (database and authentication), Google (sign-in and Drive file storage) and GitHub (website hosting). They process data on our behalf. We do not sell your data.

Storage and retention

Workspace data is stored in Supabase and uploaded files in Google Drive. We keep your data while your account is active in the workspace. When a person is removed from the team, they lose access immediately. Their content stays in the workspace unless the owner deletes it.

Security

Access is controlled by Google sign-in and database-level permissions so each person sees only the spaces and items they are allowed to see. Connections use HTTPS. Credentials for Google Drive are held as server-side secrets and are never exposed to the browser.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us at the address below.
  • You can delete files and items you created inside Teamspace.
  • You can review or remove Google account access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

Teamspace is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of Teamspace after a change means you accept the updated policy.
